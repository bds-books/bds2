## About the book

*Advanced Basketball Data Science: With Applications in R* builds on *Basketball Data Science: With Applications in R*. It explores how advanced statistical methods, machine learning, and computer vision can help us understand basketball through data.

The book examines the game at several levels: from lineups and decisive moments to player movement, shot trajectories, and scoring probabilities. Its examples connect questions from the court with reproducible analyses in **R**.

### Topics at a glance

| Games and decisions | Movement and space | Shooting and performance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lineups, game segments, and clutch performance | Tracking data, player movement, and pose estimation | Ball trajectories, shot maps, and scoring probability models |

The book is intended for analysts, coaches, researchers, and students interested in connecting quantitative methods with basketball insight.

## About this repository

This repository is dedicated to the companion materials for the book, including datasets, R code, and supporting functions to help readers follow and reproduce its analyses.
